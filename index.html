01Identity & Commitment

Rooted in faith, rooted in this island.

A Compassion-assisted project of Sena PEFA Church, serving Suba Central since 2017.

Historical Background

PEFA Mfangano Child Development Centre (CDC) is a Compassion-assisted project located in Homa Bay County, within Suba Central Sub-County at Sena Centre, within PEFA Cathedral Sena. The CDC is a result of a strategic partnership between Sena PEFA Church and Compassion International Kenya — dedicated to transforming lives by catering to the physical, spiritual, socio-economic and cognitive needs of children and youth in Mfangano Island and surrounding communities.

Partnership SignedJuly 1, 2017
Program LaunchedAugust 15, 2017
LocationSena Centre, PEFA Cathedral Sena
Our Mission

To holistically release needy children and youth from poverty in Jesus' name — nurturing every aspect of a child's growth, spiritually, physically, socially, emotionally, and economically, from pregnancy through young adulthood.

Our Vision

A future where every child grows into a fulfilled Christian adult who knows Christ, makes Him known, and is self-reliant — empowered to reach their full potential as fulfilled members of their communities.

Our Commitment

We are committed to the holistic development of children and youth, aiming to free them from spiritual, physical, socio-emotional, and economic poverty in Jesus' name.

Holistic Development. Lasting Change. In Jesus' Name.
