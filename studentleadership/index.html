Platform Principles

Built on trust, designed for students.

Every element of this platform is shaped around three principles that ensure our student elections remain fair, transparent, and accessible to every learner on Mfangano Island.

Secure

Designed for controlled access with structured authentication. Every vote is handled through a protected workflow to preserve the integrity of the process.

Transparent

A clear election process with straightforward information. Students know what they're voting for, who the candidates are, and how the process works.

Verified

A structured voting workflow with confirmation at every step. Each ballot moves through a deliberate process before being recorded.
