About the Platform

A system built on fairness and trust.

This digital voting platform ensures fair, secure, and transparent student elections. Every vote is encrypted, counted automatically, and displayed in real-time to authorized administrators.

One Vote Per Student

Your KE number is your ballot.

Each eligible voter uses a unique 3-digit KE number (001–550) to authenticate. Once your ballot is submitted, the system locks your ID to prevent duplicate voting — preserving the integrity of every election.

Range: KE-001 through KE-550

End-to-end encrypted

Every ballot is protected from submission through to final tally.

Real-time results

Aggregated counts update live on the secure administrator dashboard.

Mobile-friendly

Cast your vote from any device — phone, tablet, or computer.

Tamper-proof logs

Every action is recorded for full institutional transparency.

The Voting Process

Six deliberate steps.

From your first visit to the final tally — every action in the election workflow is designed to be clear, deliberate, and verifiable.

01

Start at the portal

Visit the election portal home and click "Enter Voter Portal" to begin your voting journey.

Entry point
02

Verify your identity

Enter your 3-digit KE number (001–550) to authenticate your voter eligibility. The system confirms you haven't voted before.

Authentication
03

Review candidates

For each of the 10 contested posts, review candidate profiles and select your preferred choice. Take your time — there is no rush.

10 positions
04

Confirm your selections

Review all of your choices on a summary screen before final submission. You can go back to change any selection at this stage.

Reversible
05

Submit your ballot

Click "Submit Vote" to securely record your ballot. Your KE number is then marked as voted and cannot be used again.

Final & binding
06

View live results

Administrators view real-time, aggregated results on the secure dashboard. Official results are published after the voting period closes.

Admin access only
